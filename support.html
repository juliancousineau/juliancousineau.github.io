Player support

For gameplay problems, purchases, or privacy questions, email Julian Cousineau.

julianacousineau@gmail.com

What to include

  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • The game version shown in the menu, and whether you use TestFlight.
  • The hotel and activity where the issue happened, plus the steps that led to it.
  • A screenshot or short recording if useful. Hide unrelated personal information.
  • For a purchase issue, the item name and approximate purchase time. Share an order reference only if needed; never send payment-card details or passwords.

Keep your local progress. If the game has a problem, contact support before deleting or reinstalling it. Removing app data can remove your saved hotels and consumable currency.

Common questions

How do I restore a permanent purchase?

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase, connect to the internet, and open Settings → Restore Purchases in the game. Restore is also available from the Shop’s permanent offers when supported. Wait for the store’s result before trying again.

Permanent benefits apply when Apple confirms ownership. Currency that has already been delivered is consumable and is not granted again by Restore Purchases. See Apple’s purchase recovery help.

What does Remove Ads include?

Remove Ads is a permanent benefit covering automatic ad breaks in all hotels. Reward videos remain optional. Remove Ads is sold separately from crew packs.

Do permanent crew benefits work in every hotel?

The permanent Trash, Maid, and Supply benefits apply across all hotels, including future hires where specified. Maid and Supply benefits improve the workers you hire. Hotel progress still determines which staff, rooms, and amenities are available. The Shop explains each offer before purchase.

How do offline earnings and Cash packs work?

The game calculates offline earnings from the hotel’s normal operating income, with the percentage and time cap shown in the game. Offline Manager is a permanent all-hotel benefit. A welcome-back message reports Cash already added.

Cash packs show the exact Cash amount and current hotel income duration before checkout. The store displays the localized price. Purchases are optional; their availability depends on the store and your connection.

What if a purchase is still pending?

Let the store finish the transaction and keep the game connected. A pending or unavailable message does not mean that the item was delivered. If the item is still missing, contact support with the item name and approximate time. Check your Apple purchase history before retrying a purchase.

Are TestFlight purchases real charges?

Apple In-App Purchases made in TestFlight use Apple’s sandbox and are free for testers. Test purchases do not carry over to the released App Store version. See Apple’s testing information.

Where can I manage advertising privacy?

Open Settings → Privacy Choices when the consent service makes that option available. Your device also provides privacy controls. Read our privacy policy for information about purchasing, advertising, local saves, and support messages.

Privacy requests

Use the same contact address and put “Hectic Hotel privacy” in the subject. Describe the information or request involved. Please send only the information needed for us to respond.
