Hectic Hotel

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Hectic Hotel is published by Julian Cousineau. This policy explains information used by the game, its purchasing and advertising services, and this support website.

Privacy contact: julianacousineau@gmail.com

Game dataPurchasesAdvertisingYour choicesSupport

Game progress and preferences

The game stores hotel progress, Cash and Gems, staff, room and amenity upgrades, preferences, event state, and purchase recovery records on your device. Offline earnings use saved departure times, the hotel’s earning rate, and settlement records to calculate an award and prevent duplicate payment for the same absence.

You do not need a publisher account to play. The game does not provide a publisher-operated cloud-save service. Device or platform backups are separate from the game’s local storage.

Purchases and recovery

Apple processes purchases made through Apple In-App Purchase. The game uses product, price, transaction, verification, and ownership information to deliver the correct item, recover interrupted transactions, and restore permanent benefits. The game does not receive your Apple Account password or payment-card number. Local recovery records include transaction identifiers and verification evidence.

The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing. Its purchasing service can process purchase details, installation or session identifiers, device and operating-system information, language, and approximate country information to operate purchasing and report revenue. Purchase events may be processed even when advertising consent is declined. The information available to a service depends on its configuration and the features used.

See Apple’s App Store privacy information, Unity’s purchasing privacy overview, and Unity’s privacy policy.

Advertising and privacy choices

The game uses Google AdMob for automatic ads at eligible breaks and optional reward videos. Google’s advertising technology may process IP addresses and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, diagnostic and performance information, ad views, and interactions. This information can be used to deliver and measure ads, operate the service, and improve performance. Advertising choices and platform permissions affect the information available and the ads served.

Google’s User Messaging Platform is used to request choices when required by the configured consent service. When available, open the game’s Settings → Privacy Choices to review those choices. Google advertising choices do not automatically change information required to operate Apple or Unity purchasing.

Remove Ads removes automatic ad breaks in all hotels. Optional reward videos remain your choice. Buying Remove Ads does not mean that every third-party service stops processing information.

Read the Google privacy policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure for more detail.

Sharing a hotel picture

Share Hotel creates a picture on your device and opens the platform sharing interface. You choose whether to share it, and with which person or service. The game does not automatically upload that picture to a publisher server. Any service you choose handles the picture under its own privacy practices.

Support messages and this website

If you email support, the publisher receives your email address, message, and attachments you choose to provide. We use this information to respond, investigate reported problems, and keep relevant support records. Email services also process messages to deliver and store them. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or identity documents.

This website’s pages use no advertising, analytics scripts, or sign-in forms. The website host may process ordinary connection information, such as IP address, requested page, and technical logs, to deliver and secure the site.

Retention and your choices

Local progress, preferences, pictures, and purchase recovery records remain in the app’s storage for game operation. Deleting the app or its data can remove local progress. Platform backups and Apple purchase records are separate. Permanent purchases may be restored when the store confirms current ownership; already-delivered consumable currency is not granted again by Restore Purchases.

You can change available in-game advertising choices, use your device’s privacy settings, decline optional reward videos and purchases, and choose not to share pictures. Support correspondence is kept as needed to address the request and relevant follow-up or recordkeeping obligations.

Contact julianacousineau@gmail.com to ask about or request deletion of information held by the publisher. We review requests in light of applicable law, necessary recordkeeping, and verification of the request. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, please contact us so we can review it. Apple, Google, and Unity provide their own procedures for information they control.

These providers may process information in other countries, as described in their policies. Their retention periods and privacy procedures are separate from local game storage.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The current version will appear on this page with an updated date.
